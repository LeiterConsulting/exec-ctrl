Distinguish inspected policy from expected-but-inaccessible sources. Record each
unresolved conflict, affected action and responsible decision-maker. Keep unrelated
allowed work moving. Add exception authority, scope, expiry and compensating control
where the organization's process permits it. Do not convert a missing source into
a made-up rule or compliance claim.
